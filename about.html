About Us

Seerat Welfare Trust (SWT) is an emerging development organisation committed to advancing inclusive, equitable and sustainable development. We seek to strengthen people’s capabilities, expand opportunities and support communities in building more resilient and self-reliant futures.

Who We Are

An Emerging Organisation. An Experienced Development Perspective.

We believe meaningful development goes beyond access to services and resources. It also requires people to have the knowledge, skills and confidence to make informed decisions, exercise greater agency and participate fully in social and economic life. We see people not simply as recipients of support, but as active participants in shaping their futures and contributing to the progress of their communities.

01

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to contribute to a more inclusive and equitable India where individuals and communities have greater opportunities to learn, participate and thrive with dignity. We aim to support initiatives that respond to barriers affecting education, access to essential services, economic participation and social inclusion, particularly among underserved communities.

02

Our Approach

Our approach centres on local needs, community participation and collaboration. We seek to strengthen individual capabilities, connect people with relevant opportunities and work with institutions and partners to develop initiatives that are responsive to local contexts. Continuous learning and accountability will guide how we design, implement and improve our work.

Seerat Welfare Trust draws on the complementary professional experience of its founding team across social development and related fields. This experience provides a foundation for programme development, stakeholder engagement, institutional partnerships and organisational strengthening.

As an emerging organisation, SWT is focused on establishing the systems, partnerships and practices needed for responsible growth. We place importance on integrity, accountability, community responsiveness and continuous improvement, recognising that institutional credibility must be built through consistent and responsible practice.

We seek to work alongside communities, government institutions, civil society organisations, foundations and corporate partners to connect shared priorities with relevant expertise and opportunities. Through these collaborations, we aim to build a credible, learning-oriented institution that translates community needs into responsive initiatives and strengthens pathways towards inclusive development.

Our Story

Turning Access into Agency. Building Pathways to Dignity.

Seerat Welfare Trust was founded on a belief that every individual deserves the opportunity to shape their future with dignity. We believe that meaningful development begins when people are able to recognise their potential, make informed choices and participate in decisions that affect their lives.

Across communities, barriers to education, financial knowledge, economic participation and essential services can restrict opportunities and limit individual potential. Addressing these challenges calls for an approach that looks beyond immediate needs and recognises the importance of people’s aspirations, capabilities and ability to shape their own futures.

Our Purpose

This belief informs the purpose behind Seerat Welfare Trust: to contribute to a society where people have greater opportunities to learn, participate and pursue a better quality of life. We aspire to support pathways that connect knowledge with action, access with opportunity, and individual progress with stronger communities.

Why Seerat?

Because Character Shapes the Change We Create.

In Urdu, “Seerat” refers to a person’s inner character, moral qualities and the values reflected in their conduct. It speaks to the principles that shape our choices, guide our relationships and influence how we fulfil our responsibilities.

At Seerat Welfare Trust, this meaning reflects our belief that meaningful development is about more than the opportunities we create; it is also about the values that guide our actions. Integrity, compassion, dignity and responsibility are essential to building trust, fostering inclusion and contributing to lasting social change.

Across communities, barriers to education, financial security, dignified livelihoods, healthcare and equal opportunities can limit people’s potential. Addressing these challenges requires approaches that respect human dignity, strengthen individual capabilities and enable people to participate more fully in shaping their own futures.

We aspire to work alongside communities and development partners to advance locally relevant, inclusive and sustainable initiatives. Guided by our values, we seek to contribute to a society where individuals have greater opportunities to realise their potential and communities can grow stronger and more resilient.

For us, Seerat is more than a name. It is a guiding philosophy that reminds us that the way we work matters as much as the change we seek to create. Our commitment is to pursue that change with integrity, purpose and respect for every individual.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

An inclusive, equitable and self-reliant India where every individual has the opportunity to realise their potential and live with dignity.

Our Mission

To turn access into agency by equipping underserved communities with the capabilities and opportunities to lead healthier, more equitable, resilient and sustainable lives.

SWT · Theory of Change

From Community Needs to Sustainable Change

We believe lasting social change begins with understanding people's needs, aspirations and lived experiences. When communities are meaningfully engaged, equipped with relevant knowledge and capabilities, and connected to appropriate services and opportunities, they are better positioned to make informed decisions and take positive action.

SWT intends to work through collaborative, community-centred initiatives that strengthen individual capabilities, encourage positive practices and build pathways towards greater well-being, resilience and self-reliance.

Our Commitment

Building with Integrity. Growing Through Partnership.

At Seerat Welfare Trust (SWT), we believe meaningful development begins with the values we hold and is reflected in the actions we take. Integrity, compassion and respect for human dignity must guide not only what we seek to achieve, but also how we work with people and communities.

As an emerging organisation, we are committed to building strong institutional foundations, developing meaningful partnerships and creating initiatives that are inclusive, responsible and responsive to community needs. We recognise that trust is earned through consistent action, transparent practices and a willingness to listen, learn and improve.

Our commitment is guided by six principles:

Integrity

Upholding honesty, fairness and respect in our decisions, relationships and actions.

Inclusion

Promoting equitable opportunities and recognising the dignity, voices and aspirations of diverse communities.

Accountability

Taking responsibility for our commitments, the resources entrusted to us and the results of our work.

Partnership

Collaborating with communities, institutions and development partners to advance shared priorities and strengthen collective impact.

Innovation

Exploring relevant ideas, technologies and approaches that respond to evolving community needs and improve programme effectiveness.

Sustainability

Supporting approaches that create lasting value, strengthen community resilience and contribute to long-term well-being.

→

We aspire to put these principles into practice by listening to communities, designing locally relevant initiatives, monitoring progress and learning from experience. Through evidence-informed decisions, constructive partnerships and continuous improvement, we aim to strengthen programme quality and remain responsive to the people and communities at the heart of our work.

→

For Seerat Welfare Trust, these commitments extend beyond written principles. We aspire to ensure that our inner values are reflected in responsible action and that respect for human dignity shapes our decisions and relationships. We believe lasting change depends not only on what we do, but also on the character, purpose and principles that guide how we do it.

Md Raza Ur RahmanFounder & Chairperson

Founder’s Message

The Values Behind Our Purpose

Seerat Welfare Trust was founded on a simple belief: the values we carry should be reflected in how we live, how we treat others and how we contribute to society.

The name “Seerat” holds a special personal significance for me. It is my mother’s name and, in Urdu, refers to inner character, moral values and conduct. The values she instilled in us—integrity, compassion, humility and a sense of responsibility towards others—continue to shape my understanding of what it means to lead a life of purpose.

When I envisioned Seerat Welfare Trust, I wanted this personal inspiration to grow into something larger: an institution guided by these enduring values and committed to advancing human dignity, inclusive development and meaningful community participation.

I believe meaningful development goes beyond creating opportunities. It means ensuring that people are treated with dignity, that communities have a voice in decisions affecting their lives, and that institutions work with integrity, transparency and accountability. These principles will guide how Seerat Welfare Trust engages with communities, builds partnerships and translates its mission into meaningful action.

As we build the Trust, my commitment is to nurture an institution that remains true to its founding values, learns from the communities it serves and works collaboratively with partners to create sustainable and inclusive opportunities for underserved populations. I aspire for Seerat Welfare Trust to become a trusted institution that contributes meaningfully to a more inclusive, equitable and self-reliant India.

For me, “Seerat” is more than a name; it is a reminder that the character of an institution is reflected in the values it upholds, the people it serves and the responsibility with which it works towards change.

Md Raza Ur RahmanFounder & Chairperson, Seerat Welfare Trust
