Privacy

Privacy Notice

How Seerat Welfare Trust handles the information you share with us through this website.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form on our website, we collect the details you choose to give us:

  • your full name and email address;
  • your phone number, if you provide it;
  • the topic you select (for example, volunteering or partnering with us); and
  • the message you write.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use it

We use this information only to read and respond to your enquiry, and to keep a record of our correspondence with you.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for their own use. Enquiries are delivered to us by our form provider, Formspree, which processes the message solely to pass it on to us by email.

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us at any time by emailing swt@seerat.org.in. We will act on your request promptly.
