Who We Are

Seerat Welfare Trust believes that sustainable development begins with people — their capabilities, aspirations and opportunities.

An Emerging Organisation. An Experienced Development Perspective.

  • The Trust seeks to contribute to a more inclusive, equitable and self-reliant India by supporting initiatives that strengthen individual capabilities, promote informed decision-making and improve access to opportunities.
  • Drawing on professional experience in social development and related fields, SWT aims to build meaningful institutional partnerships and develop community-centred initiatives that respond to local needs and contribute to sustainable change.
  • Our approach emphasises dignity, inclusion, accountability and collaboration, with a commitment to learning from communities and strengthening programme quality.

Why Seerat?

Because Every Life Deserves the Opportunity to Thrive.

Across communities, barriers to education, financial security, dignified livelihoods, healthcare and equal opportunities continue to limit potential. Addressing these challenges requires more than access to services; it calls for stronger capabilities, inclusive systems and the agency to shape one’s own future.

Seerat Welfare Trust believes sustainable development begins with people—their aspirations, capabilities and ability to create change in their lives and communities.

We aspire to work alongside communities and development partners to advance inclusive, locally relevant and sustainable solutions that expand opportunities, strengthen agency and promote self-reliance.

For Seerat, this means creating pathways to greater dignity, resilience and a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

When people recognise their potential and have the opportunity to realise it, stronger communities become possible.

Our Focus Areas

Six Areas of Work. One Shared Purpose.

Our six interconnected focus areas reflect our belief that sustainable development requires more than access to services. It calls for stronger capabilities, greater agency, equitable opportunities and collective action to address the barriers that limit human potential.

Through community-centred initiatives, strategic partnerships and evidence-informed approaches, SWT aims to support individuals and communities in building the knowledge, skills and resilience needed to create lasting positive change.

Our approach will be guided by community priorities, local contexts and opportunities for collaboration, ensuring that future initiatives remain relevant, inclusive and responsive to the needs of the people we seek to serve.

Global Alignment

Contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals

Seerat Welfare Trust aligns its development priorities with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), a global framework for advancing human well-being, reducing inequalities and building a more inclusive and sustainable future.

Our Alignment with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Through our six interconnected focus areas, we aim to contribute to relevant SDGs by strengthening individual and community capabilities, expanding access to opportunities and encouraging collaborative, locally relevant solutions.

Our approach recognises that sustainable development requires progress across education, economic opportunity, gender equality, health, environmental sustainability and inclusive institutions.

As our programmes evolve, we intend to identify relevant SDG targets, integrate appropriate indicators into programme design and use evidence to assess our contribution to these shared development priorities.

Our commitment is to translate global development priorities into meaningful, locally relevant action.
